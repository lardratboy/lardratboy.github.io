Citadel

You hold the centre. Three rings of specimens orbit you, and each ring is one row of the showroom wrapped into a circle: one seed, one trait stepping around the loop.

Inner rings soak up fire from the rings behind them. Clear the inner ring and everything closes in, with a fresh ring minted at the edge.

The orbit lance deletes every voxel that shares a symmetry orbit with what it hits. It shreds octahedral specimens and barely scratches asymmetric ones.
