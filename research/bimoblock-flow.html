Showroom specimens carried along geodesics of S³, inside the Clifford solid torus.

Flow

Along a geodesic the 2-plane span(p,v) is invariant, so two of each block's three axes are literally constant vectors in R⁴. Only the nose axis moves, and it points down the flow.
