keys and links

the camera and the view

left drag: orbit   right or shift-left drag: pan
middle drag or wheel: dolly (through walls)   two fingers: pinch, pan
WASD: move   E/Q: up/down   arrows: look   shift: ×8
alt-wheel: speed   F: freeze cut
H: show or hide the panels   T: wireframe   Y: SSAO   ?: this list
[ / ]: seed − / +   R: a random seed
drop .vdag files anywhere to open them

edit mode

B: edit mode. Esc leaves
  clay, flat ground: left break, right place, middle take a colour
    wheel or - =: block side   1-8: colour
  the kit, the shapes: a click opens a box for the shelf world
    drag an arrow: move   a ring: turn   a corner: double or halve
    drag the box: slide it over the world   click elsewhere: put it there
    Enter bakes the box   Esc closes it   shift-click drops at once
    J L spin   I K tip   U O roll   M mirror   V underside to the face
    P: the child drawn in the box, or not (?preview=0 starts with it off)
    wheel or - =: scale   G: snap 16 / voxel
    1-8: which shelf world (or the place menu)
    right click or Z: undo, and pick the last one up
    a drag anywhere else: the camera, as above
  Z / shift-Z: undo / redo   alt-wheel: speed

a gamepad

pad: L stick move, R stick look, RT/LT up/down
     A or L3 ×8, RB/LB speed ×2/÷2, Y freeze cut

the link

?world=&depth=&detail=&max=&workers=&eye=x,y,z
?world=lattice:1&lattice=R,nx,ny,nz,gap,turns,axes,align
?world=kit:1&kit=D,libs,ornaments,ground,colour
?world=caves:<seed>&depth=10..12   (scatter:, lattice:, kit: the same)
?world=clay&clay=D,lump,shape,ground   (lump 0: flat ground, or empty without ground)
?hud=1 opens with the panels   ?wire=1 with the wireframe
?quiet draws once converged   ?selftest runs streamSelftest()
?ray=off|density|0..6 (raster only by default)   ?rule=shadow|any|adaptive (shadow by default)
?density=0.27,0.025   ?stencil=0   ?raymiss=discard   ?bench   raySelftest()
?ssao=1 (or slices,steps,scale,radius)   ?ssaoshow=1   ?ssaobench
?occlusion=queries|depth (off by default)   occlusionSelftest()
