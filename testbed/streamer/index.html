Worlds as DAGs

A streaming voxel engine built on hash-consed binary DAGs: trillions of voxels in thousands of nodes, cut into 64³ chunks near the eye and drawn as instanced cubes. These are the pages that build it, use it and explain it.

The engine

Start here

Stream viewer

Fly, walk and build through worlds up to a million voxels a side. Chunks stream in from worker threads at the level of detail the distance calls for. Every generated world is here: the kit, a city with a river and harbour, a dungeon, caves, a math gallery, a universe of worlds and an endless one. You can also open .vdag files, carve blocks and drop worlds into worlds.

Apps

things to play with and tools for world files

Destruction arena

Rockets and a plasma cannon against specimens, math shapes and your own world files. Debris falls, settles and bakes back into the world, and not one voxel is lost. A wrecked arena saves as a world file, to open again here or in the stream viewer.

destroy/

DAG explorer

A world file's DAG drawn in 3D above the world it describes: a dot for each node and a line for each reference. Pick a node to see where it occurs, or a cell to trace its path to the root.

explore/

vdag → glb

Opens a .vdag and makes its surface at every level of detail, the way the viewer draws it. Save any level as a glTF binary for Blender or a game engine.

export/

glb, ply, vox → vdag

Turns glTF meshes, PLY meshes and point clouds, and MagicaVoxel models into world files in the browser, with their textures or palettes baked into colour. Nothing leaves your machine.

convert/

vdb → vdag

Turns OpenVDB volumes and points (level sets, Houdini smoke and VDB points, Blender fluid caches) into world files: the inside or the density over a threshold made solid, a point grid's points made voxels, and a colour grid or the points' Cd baked onto the surface.
